Product 1GCSS, Geographic Coverage Support System

15,800 settlements.

Polio campaigns had missed every one. My team built the map that found them, with WHO, NEOC, and the Gates Foundation. A campaign only works if it reaches every child.

  1. We mapped boundaries for 6,000+ administrative areas.
  2. We put 69,000+ vaccinators on a live map.
  3. We built detection that flags settlements no team reached.
  4. Follow-up teams went straight to the flags.

5,000+ children

vaccinated because a dot turned amber. WHO adopted GCSS to monitor polio campaigns.

Product 2Team support centre detection

The reports said teams started here. The GPS said otherwise.

So we let the GPS decide.

  • An ML model reads each team's GPS trace and finds where its day really starts.
  • Geofences timestamp every arrival and departure.
  • The gap between declared and detected locations showed where reporting was off.

95%+ of team support centres found automatically

Product 3VaxiSure smart cold chain

A warm vaccine looks exactly like a cold one.

So we built a carrier that reports on itself. It logs temperature, location, and every time someone opens the lid.

  • Pilots caught several cold chain breaches in time to act.
  • The rollout proposal covers 180 devices in 36 districts.

Up to 25% less potential vaccine wastage

Product 4DISP, Digital Information System for Polio

Every tally mark was a child.

Campaign counts lived on paper tally sheets. Paper is slow to collect and hard to check. We moved the count onto offline-first Android and web apps.

  • I ran research with vaccinators, area in-charges, and union council medical officers.
  • We piloted, listened, and reworked the backlog after every round.
  • The system is now scaling to a national rollout.

40%+ better data timeliness and accuracy in pilot districts
